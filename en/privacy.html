Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

HALE MAKAWAO ("we", "our", or "the property") sets forth the following privacy policy regarding the handling of personal information on our official website halemakawao.com (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

  • Information collected via cookies and similar technologies, including page views, referrer, device information, and time spent on the Site.
  • Personal information such as name, email address, and inquiry content provided through contact forms.

2. Use of Analytics Tools

The Site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, for the purpose of improving the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect traffic data. IP addresses are anonymized (anonymize_ip), and Google Signals is disabled.

Collected data is used solely for analyzing site usage and is not used to identify individuals. To opt out of Google's data collection, please install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Cookies and Consent

On your first visit, the Site displays a banner requesting your consent to the use of cookies. If you select "Decline", Google Analytics will not measure your activity. Your consent status is stored in your browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

4. Purpose of Use

  • To understand site usage and improve our services.
  • To respond to inquiries.
  • To comply with applicable laws.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

We will not disclose your personal information to third parties except with your consent or as required by law.

6. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, or Deletion

If you request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of your retained personal data, we will respond promptly within a reasonable scope after verifying your identity. Please contact us via the inquiry form on this Site.

7. Changes

This Policy may be revised in response to changes in laws and regulations. The revised Policy takes effect when posted on this page.
