Daisen Ski Resort 2026–27 Season Guide

Updated: November 11, 2026 | Category: Area Guide / Ski & Snow

From the 2026–27 season, the ski area on Mt. Daisen operates under new management as Daisen Mountain Resort (大山マウンテンリゾート), formerly Daisen White Resort. The season is scheduled to open on December 18, 2026. HALE MAKAWAO is about 5 minutes by car from the slopes.

2026–27 season

  • Opening: December 18, 2026 (scheduled; may change with snow conditions)
  • Season: until late March 2027 (scheduled)
  • Hours: 8:30–16:30
  • Night skiing: none

The slopes

  • Top elevation: 1,121 m
  • Courses: 8, from beginner to advanced
  • Maximum gradient: 30°; longest run: over 1.6 km
  • Three areas: Goenzan, Nakanohara and Kokusai
  • Beginner course テクニクダス: 1,400 m long, maximum gradient 11°, in the Goenzan area, which also has a Kids Land for sledding

  • Parent-and-child ticket: ¥9,000

Lessons and rentals

  • Ski schools include Daisen Snow School and G-three. Kids lessons are available, and some lessons are offered in English
  • Ski, snowboard and wear rentals are available. Elementary school age and younger get 20% off all rental items
  • On busy days, contact the resort in advance for lessons and rentals

Access and parking

  • About 15 minutes from Yonago IC on the Yonago Expressway
  • About 50 minutes by car from Yonago Kitaro Airport
  • From JR Yonago Station, about 50 minutes by bus to the Daisenji stop
  • Winter parking: ¥1,000 per car, about 1,500 spaces near the slopes
  • Snow tires or chains are required in winter

Staying at HALE MAKAWAO

  • About 5 minutes by car to the slopes
  • Entire house for one group per day, five rooms, about 200 m², bookable for up to 14 guests on Airbnb
  • The day-use hot spring Goen-yu-in is in the Daisenji area near the slopes
