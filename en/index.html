Concept

A forest retreat
where time stands still.

Nestled in the quiet woods at the foot of Mt. Daisen, HALE MAKAWAO is an exclusive, one-group-per-day private villa in Tottori Prefecture, western Japan. Mt. Daisen — often called the "Mt. Fuji of western Japan" — is a sacred volcano surrounded by ancient beech forests, ski slopes, and pristine nature.

There is no lavish decor or noisy entertainment here. Instead, you will find a wall of forest views and the gentle singing of birds.

Because you rent the entire house, families with children can relax completely without worrying about neighbors. Leave your watch behind and enjoy the luxury of doing absolutely nothing — or venture out to nearby Sakaiminato (the Mizuki Shigeru Road yokai town) and the world-renowned Adachi Museum of Art.

Things to Do Near HALE MAKAWAO

Mt. Daisen Skiing & Hiking

Mt. Daisen ski resort is just 15 minutes from the villa — ideal for a ski lodge stay in western Japan. In summer and autumn, the beech forest trails offer some of Tottori's most spectacular hiking.

Sakaiminato – Mizuki Shigeru Road

Approx. 30 minutes by car. Sakaiminato is a beloved port town famous for its yokai (spirit) statues lining the shopping street — a must-see for families and manga fans traveling in Japan.

Adachi Museum of Art

Approx. 40 minutes by car. Ranked the No. 1 Japanese garden in Japan by the Journal of Japanese Gardening for over 20 consecutive years. A world-class destination for art and landscape lovers visiting Tottori.

Tottori Sand Dunes

Japan's only large-scale sand dunes, about 50 minutes by car — a dramatic and unexpected natural wonder that surprises every first-time visitor to Tottori Prefecture.

Yonago & Daisen Craft Beer (Daisen G Beer)

We offer round-trip transfers to Daisen G Beer, a local brewery whose Weizen was recognized as one of the world's best. A perfect evening out for craft beer lovers — no driving needed.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is HALE MAKAWAO from Yonago Airport (YGJ)?

Approximately 25 minutes by car. Yonago Airport (Yonago Kitaro Airport, IATA: YGJ) is the nearest airport, with direct flights from Taipei (Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport, TPE) twice a week as of May 2025. Car rental is recommended for getting around the Mt. Daisen area.

How many guests can stay at once?

Up to 6 guests. HALE MAKAWAO accommodates families and small groups. The entire house is yours — bedrooms include a Japanese tatami room, a double room, and a twin room.

Is this a good base for skiing at Mt. Daisen?

Yes. The Mt. Daisen ski resort is approximately 15 minutes from the villa by car, making HALE MAKAWAO one of the closest private lodging options for skiers. The ski season typically runs from December through March.

How do I book? Is it available on Airbnb?

Yes, HALE MAKAWAO is listed on Airbnb. Click the "Book on Airbnb" button at the top of the page to check availability and make a reservation. Nightly rates start from ¥40,000.

Are pets allowed?

We are sorry, but pets are not permitted at HALE MAKAWAO.
